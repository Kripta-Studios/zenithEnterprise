# R1 and packet mechanism status

The historical R1 trial uses human-labeled QASPER and ContractNLI development data, equal 1,100 BGE tokenizer source-token budgets, fixed legacy leaders plus grouping, and a separately re-embedded structural index. The alternate index is real within that isolated trial; it did not modify production. On QASPER, complete evidence was 53.40% current chunks, 56.31% grouping, 52.91% re-embedded. On ContractNLI it was 69.06%, 72.64%, 74.59%. Top-1 overlap fell under grouping in both datasets. The two datasets therefore do not justify a universal index promotion. See [R1 report](../../backend/eval/reports/evidence-v3-r1-broad-2026-09-26.md).

The historical corrected packet selector compared the same current chunks and equal source-context caps. Dense QASPER was 53.40% with or without packets; TEI QASPER fell from 50.97% to 50.49% (one lost evidence span). ContractNLI dense and TEI complete-evidence rates were unchanged at 69.06% and 77.04%. Counterevidence added no complete-evidence gain. These are negative/null observations and remain published. See [packet report](../../backend/eval/reports/evidence-v3-packets-2026-09-26.md).

This hardening pass extracted shared source-interval and scoring helpers so packet evaluation no longer imports optional R1 trials. Frozen 176-case and descriptive 416-case historical outcomes were identical after offline rescoring. No new representative enterprise corpus with independent gold evidence, diagnostic stress set, full-prompt generator-token comparison, or newly frozen locked R1/packet test was completed. Both components remain optional and off; the historical contribution stays intact. A future test must freeze representative and stress samples separately, keep source identity, compare top-k/neighbor/bundle/counterevidence under equal complete budgets, and measure generated answers without selecting a favorable cohort after inspection.
